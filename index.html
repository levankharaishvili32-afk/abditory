Custom orders

Custom is not an add-on here — it is the whole point. Most of what leaves the workshop was drawn for one person.

  1. 01

    Message

    Send us a DM with a reference, a sketch, or just a description. Rough ideas are fine — that is where most pairs start.

  2. 02

    Design

    We settle the shape, height, materials and hardware together, take your measurements, and confirm the price before anything begins.

  3. 03

    Made

    Your pair is cut, lasted and finished by hand in the Tbilisi workshop. You get progress photos along the way.

  4. 04

    Shipped

    Packed and sent with tracking, anywhere in the world. Delivery time depends on where you are.

Typical turnaround 3–5 weeks

From confirmed design to shipped. Complex pieces take longer — we will tell you before you commit.

The workshop

About

ABDITORY is a small workshop in Tbilisi making gothic footwear and accessories to order. Nothing here is mass-produced. Every piece is built for one person — chosen height, chosen hardware, chosen finish — and made by hand, one at a time.

An abditory is a place where things are hidden. What we make is for people who would rather not blend in.
